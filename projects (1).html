Jamaica's Healing Roots

A feature documentary exploring the medicinal plant knowledge held by Jamaica's elder herbalists and healers — traditions passed down through generations, largely undocumented on film until now.

The production includes interviews with University of the West Indies (UWI Mona) lecturers alongside community herbalists, filmed on location across the island.

Filming schedule

Dec 8–9 Kingston area, including UWI Mona · Dec 10 Blue Mountains · Dec 11–12 Portland · Dec 13 Trelawny · Dec 14–15 St. Elizabeth
