About Us · St. Ann, Jamaica

Rooted in Culture. Driven by Purpose.

Our journey

Stories with meaning and memory.

Caribbean Storytime Studios is an independent film and media company founded in St. Ann, Jamaica by producer and director Lavern Bernard, out of a deep love for the Caribbean and a commitment to authentic storytelling.

From the mountains to the sea, from ancestral traditions to modern innovation, we shine a light on voices and experiences that deserve to be seen, heard, and remembered. Our current production, Jamaica's Healing Roots, follows the island's elder herbalists across the Blue Mountains, Portland, Trelawny, and St. Elizabeth.

Our stories. Our time. One film at a time.

Our Mission

To produce high-quality films that preserve Caribbean heritage, elevate diverse voices, and inspire understanding.

Our Vision

To become a respected Caribbean media company known for excellent storytelling and meaningful impact.

Our Values

Authenticity, integrity, creativity, collaboration, respect, and excellence.

Our inspiration

The Caribbean is more than a setting. It is our source.

Every wave, melody, family tradition, landscape, and community carries a story. We honor that legacy by creating work that is visually compelling, culturally grounded, and accessible to audiences everywhere.
