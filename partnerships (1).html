Partnering to Share Caribbean Stories

Help us preserve authentic Caribbean stories and bring meaningful documentaries to audiences around the world.

Our invitation

Create lasting cultural impact with us.

We welcome partnerships with foundations, grant-making organizations, corporate sponsors, broadcasters, cultural institutions, and individuals who share our passion for preserving and sharing authentic Caribbean stories through film. Together, we can create documentaries that educate, inspire, and leave a lasting impact.
